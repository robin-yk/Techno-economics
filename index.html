Assumed operating envelopes and lowest points found

Boundary of this comparison. The model prices efficiency and the assumed temperature–residence-time envelopes. It does not yet size Joule-specific electrical equipment, model temperature profiles, or assign route-specific capital and element-replacement costs. The result is a screening value of access to the wider envelope, not a detailed reactor design estimate.

What the tool takes from the paper

The published cost of $680 per tonne used as the benchmark marker, the total bare module cost anchor of $126.8 million at 610 thousand tonnes per year, and the ratio of total overnight cost to bare module cost. Everything else, including the kinetics, the separation duties and the emission factors, is built here and is stated on the balances tab.
